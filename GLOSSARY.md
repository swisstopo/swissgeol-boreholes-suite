# swissgeol boreholes

Records, edits and publishes boreholes and their stratigraphy for the Swiss geological survey.

## Language

### Location

**Location info**:
The country, canton and municipality a borehole lies in, always derived from its original coordinates and never entered by hand.
_Avoid_: Address, place

**Municipality**:
The municipality whose boundaries contain the borehole today, not the one at the time of drilling.
_Avoid_: City, Gemeinde, commune

**Canton**:
The canton whose boundaries contain the borehole today.

**Country**:
The country whose boundaries contain the borehole today; Switzerland or Liechtenstein for points inside the swisstopo boundary data.
